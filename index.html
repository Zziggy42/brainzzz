The twist · one coin per run

5 pieces. 1 computer.
Launch a coin, inside a zombie game.

Somewhere in the cinema, someone left a computer in pieces. Find all five while the horde hunts you, build it on the workbench, and you can launch your own coin from inside the game, then watch it pump live on the big screen while your squad holds the line.

  1. 01Scavenge

    A Motherboard, Monitor, Keyboard, Hard Drive and Wi-Fi Antenna are hidden across the map, in different rooms every run. Buy doors and push deeper to reach them.

  2. 02Build

    Carry all five to the workbench in the Maker Space and press F. The Launch Terminal boots up.

  3. 03Launch

    Press P, name your coin, pick a ticker, drop in an image and hit Create coin, just like a real launchpad, mid-round.

  4. 04Pump it

    Your coin goes live on the theater screen. Kills pump it, downs dump it, and killing The Brute sends it to the moon. How high can your squad take it?

The coin you launch in the game exists only for that run. It isn't a real token and nothing is bought or sold.
